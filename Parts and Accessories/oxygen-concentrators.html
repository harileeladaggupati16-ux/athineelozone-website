What is an Oxygen Concentrator?

An oxygen concentrator is a device that separates oxygen from ambient air, delivering a high-purity oxygen supply. It uses Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) technology to remove nitrogen and other gases, producing oxygen with purity levels up to 95%.

Oxygen concentrators are widely used in hospitals, healthcare facilities, industrial processes, laboratories, aquaculture, ozone generation systems, and water treatment plants where continuous oxygen supply is required.
