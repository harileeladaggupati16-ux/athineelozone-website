What is an Ozone Destruct Unit?

An Ozone Destruct Unit is a specialized system designed to safely remove residual ozone from gas streams before the gas is released into the surrounding environment or discharged into downstream equipment.

Ozone is an effective oxidizing agent used in water treatment, wastewater treatment, swimming pools, aquaculture, food processing and industrial applications. After the treatment process, excess ozone must be properly destroyed to prevent unwanted ozone exposure.

Ozone destruct systems convert residual ozone into oxygen through a controlled destruction process, helping provide safer and more environmentally responsible operation.

How Does an Ozone Destruct Unit Work?

During ozone treatment, a portion of the generated ozone may remain in the off-gas after the treatment process.

The ozone-containing gas is directed into the ozone destruct unit. The destruct system promotes the conversion of ozone into oxygen before the treated gas is discharged.

  • Ozone-containing off-gas enters the destruct unit.
  • Residual ozone passes through the destruction media or catalytic system.
  • Ozone is converted into oxygen.
  • Treated gas is discharged from the system.

Safety Note
Ozone is a powerful oxidizing gas and should not be released into occupied areas. Ozone destruct equipment should be correctly sized, installed and maintained according to the application and applicable safety requirements.

Ozone Destruct System Installation

Ozone destruct units are normally installed on the off-gas line from an ozone contact tank, reactor, degassing system or other ozone treatment equipment.

Proper system sizing is important to ensure that the destruct unit can handle the expected gas flow and ozone concentration.

  • Install the unit in an accessible location.
  • Provide suitable gas piping connections.
  • Avoid excessive pressure drop in the off-gas line.
  • Provide appropriate ventilation.
  • Inspect the system periodically.

Maintenance Tips

  • Inspect inlet and outlet connections regularly.
  • Check for gas leakage around fittings and piping.
  • Monitor ozone destruct performance.
  • Inspect or replace destruct media when required.
  • Keep ventilation openings clean.
  • Follow the manufacturer's maintenance schedule.
Maintenance Reminder
Always isolate the ozone source and follow appropriate safety procedures before servicing an ozone destruct system.
