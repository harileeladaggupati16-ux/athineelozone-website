What is a Nano Bubble Generator?

A Nano Bubble Generator is an advanced water treatment system designed to produce extremely small gas bubbles in water. These microscopic bubbles can remain suspended in water for extended periods and provide efficient gas transfer.

Nano bubble technology can improve oxygen transfer, water quality, circulation and treatment efficiency. Depending on the gas used, nano bubbles can be generated for oxygenation, ozone treatment, carbon dioxide applications and other specialized processes.

Nano Bubble Generators are widely used in aquaculture, agriculture, water treatment, wastewater treatment, industrial processes and environmental applications.

How Does a Nano Bubble Generator Work?

A Nano Bubble Generator introduces gas into a water stream and uses specialized hydraulic or mechanical technology to create very small bubbles.

The generated bubbles have a large surface-area-to-volume ratio, which can improve gas transfer between the gas and liquid phases.

The treated water is continuously circulated through the system, allowing the nano bubbles to interact with the surrounding water.

  • Water enters the circulation system.
  • Gas such as oxygen or ozone is introduced.
  • The generator creates ultra-fine bubbles.
  • Gas is efficiently transferred into the water.
  • Treated water is returned to the tank or process.
