What are Ozone Plates?

Ozone plates are one of the most important components in an ozone generator. They generate ozone gas using high-voltage electrical discharge across specially coated ceramic plates. Ozone plates are widely used in air purification, odor removal, water treatment, industrial sanitation, and commercial ozone systems.

High-quality ozone plates provide excellent ozone output, long service life, energy efficiency, and stable performance for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Maintenance Tips

  • Clean ozone plates regularly to remove dust.
  • Replace damaged ceramic plates immediately.
  • Keep the generator dry and moisture-free.
  • Ensure proper airflow for cooling.
  • Inspect electrical connections periodically.
Important
Always disconnect the power supply before cleaning or replacing ozone plates.
