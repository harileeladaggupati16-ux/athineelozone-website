What is a Venturi Injector?

A Venturi injector is a fluid injection device used to introduce ozone or other gases into a flowing water stream.

It works by creating a pressure difference as water passes through a specially designed narrow section. This pressure difference creates suction, allowing ozone gas to be drawn into the water stream.

Venturi injectors are widely used in ozone water treatment, swimming pools, aquaculture, wastewater treatment and industrial water treatment systems.

How Does a Venturi Injector Work?

Simple and efficient ozone injection process

1

Water Flow

Water enters the Venturi injector through the inlet connection.

2

Pressure Difference

Water velocity increases through the narrow Venturi section, creating a pressure difference.

3

Ozone Injection

The pressure difference draws ozone gas into the water stream for treatment.
