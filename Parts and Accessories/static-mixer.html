Advanced Static Mixing Technology

Our static mixers are designed for continuous inline mixing applications where uniform distribution of ozone, chemicals or other fluids is required.

Internal mixing elements create controlled turbulence and flow patterns to improve mixing efficiency without the need for external mechanical drives.

  • Efficient inline mixing

How Static Mixer Works

A simple continuous-flow process for achieving efficient mixing.

01

Fluid Entry

Water or process fluid enters the static mixer through the pipeline.

02

Injection

Ozone, chemical or other treatment media is introduced into the flow.

03

Mixing

Internal elements create flow patterns that promote uniform mixing.

04

Output

The treated and uniformly mixed fluid exits the mixer for the next process stage.
