Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ozone test kits.

What is an ozone test kit?

An ozone test kit is a testing tool used to determine the concentration of ozone in water during ozone treatment processes.

Why should ozone levels be monitored?

Monitoring helps operators verify treatment performance and maintain the desired ozone concentration for the application.

Where can ozone test kits be used?

They can be used in drinking water treatment, industrial water treatment, swimming pools, aquaculture and laboratory applications.

Are the test kits portable?

Many ozone test kits are designed in a compact format, making them convenient for field and plant testing.
