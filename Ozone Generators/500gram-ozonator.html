Product Overview

The 500 Gram Ozonator is a high-performance industrial ozone generator designed for heavy-duty continuous ozone production in demanding industrial environments. It provides exceptional oxidation, sterilization, odor removal, and water purification for very high-flow applications.

Built with advanced corona discharge ozone cells, PLC-based digital controls, and a heavy-duty stainless steel cabinet, the system delivers maximum efficiency, reliable performance, and long operational life.
