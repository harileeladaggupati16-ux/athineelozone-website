Ozone vs Chlorine

Understand the differences between ozone and chlorine in water disinfection, oxidation, safety, and environmental impact.

Why Compare Ozone & Chlorine?

Choosing the right disinfection technology matters.

Powerful Oxidation

Ozone is a very strong oxidizing agent that reacts rapidly with many contaminants.

Effective Disinfection

Both ozone and chlorine are used to reduce harmful microorganisms in water treatment.

Eco Friendly

Ozone naturally breaks down into oxygen after treatment.

Benefits of Ozone

Improves Water Quality

Helps improve water clarity, taste, and odor.

Environment Friendly

Decomposes into oxygen without leaving long-term chemical residue.

Industrial Applications

Suitable for water treatment, food processing, and wastewater treatment.
